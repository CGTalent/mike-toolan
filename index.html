The story

On air. On stage.
In the room.

Currently presenting Hits Radio’s national Drivetime show alongside Gemma Atkinson, reaching 1.8 million listeners across the UK, Mike previously spent an incredible 18 years at the helm of Manchester’s hugely successful Key 103 Breakfast Show.

Mike’s television credits include appearances on ITV’s This Morning and Channel 5.

But Mike is just as comfortable in front of a packed stadium as Mike is behind a radio microphone. From major festivals and awards ceremonies to corporate events and high-profile charity fundraisers, Mike has spent years entertaining audiences of every size.

Mike’s credits include hosting Manchester United’s historic Treble-winning celebration, David Beckham’s UNICEF testimonial at Old Trafford, The Brit Fest, the Manchester United Players’ Awards and events for Sir Alex Ferguson’s charities.
